One Key. Everything you need to know and do.

Your Kado Key is your personal health plan, written in plain words and signed by a Kado doctor. It has three parts.

K
Knowledge
Where you stand. What needs attention in your report, what is already healthy, and a short note from your doctor.
E
Execution
What to do. Supplements, doctor visits and medicines, the tests to do next, food, movement, sleep and stress. Each step says which values it should help.
Y
Your must-knows
What to keep in mind. The warning signs that mean “see a doctor in person now”, and when Kado will check in with you.
Kado stays with you after your Key, with regular check-ins on WhatsApp and help when your next report comes in.

Every Key is signed by a doctor

Kado is run by doctors. AI helps us read your report and listen to your story, but a registered Kado doctor checks every Key, edits it where needed, and signs it with their name and registration number. Nothing reaches you without that sign-off.

Common questions

Which reports can I send?
Blood reports from any lab in India, as a PDF or a clear photo. If your report is old or unclear, Kado will tell you and ask for a newer one.
How long does it take?
The call takes about 15 minutes. Your signed Key reaches you within 24 hours.
Does Kado replace my doctor?
No. Kado helps you understand your report and plan your next steps, and your Key is signed by a Kado doctor. For anything urgent, or if you feel unwell, see a doctor in person.
Which languages?
English, Hindi and Hinglish.
Who can use Kado?
Adults aged 18 and over in India.
